## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in AI at Fudan University, advised by [Prof. Zenglin Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gF0H9nEAAAAJ). I previously spent a year at KAIST and completed my master’s degree at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (supervised by Prof. Zenglin Xu). My master’s research focused on graph neural networks; I am currently transitioning to AI for Science, with a particular interest in computational drug discovery. I also receive guidance from [Prof. Shirui Pan](https://trust-agi.github.io/author/shirui-pan/) at Griffith University.

## Research Interests

- **Graph Neural Networks:** graph representation learning, over-squashing, heterophily
- **AI4S:** agentic AI systems for medicine, computational drug discovery
