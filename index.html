Langzhang Liang.

Second-year Ph.D. student

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in a joint program between Fudan University and the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII), advised by Prof. Zenglin Xu at Fudan and Prof. Tianlei Ying at SII.

My research spans AI for Science, graph representation learning, and language agents, with a particular interest in protein design and computational drug discovery.

Previously, I spent a year at KAIST and earned my master's degree at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by Prof. Zenglin Xu. I also receive guidance from Prof. Shirui Pan at Griffith University.
